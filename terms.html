FictionCom beta terms and community rules

Updated October 1, 2026. This is a testing beta operated by @dgueye1 in Georgia, United States. Public-launch policy review remains pending.

Who can join

You must be at least 14 years old to register. Keep your login private, use accurate account information and respect other members. Use the age-appropriate features and follow applicable requirements where you live.

What you can post

Anime, manga, cosplay, collections, reactions and theories are welcome. Do not post exposed nipples or genitals, full nudity, explicit sexual activity, sexual content involving minors or characters depicted as minors, harassment, hate, threats, personal information without permission, spam or stolen content. Covered exaggerated anime proportions are allowed. Label spoilers with the series and episode or chapter.

Your uploads

Only upload material you have permission to share. Uploads publish without automated content review. The moderator may remove content or suspend accounts that break the rules. Reporting a post sends a private report for review; it does not automatically remove the post. Use Support to appeal a decision. You retain your ownership; posting lets FictionCom store and display the content according to your privacy settings.

Chat and feedback

The community rules also apply to private messages. Do not send harassment, threats, spam or inappropriate content. Users can decline message requests and block senders. Use Support to report an abusive conversation; do not share sensitive information in feedback.

Membership

When activated, membership includes one 7-day free trial per account. A card is required through Stripe Checkout before the trial begins. After 7 days, your card is automatically charged $5 USD per month unless you cancel before the trial ends. You can cancel at any time to stop future charges. Cancellation during the trial preserves access until the trial ends without a subscription charge. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Manage subscription allows you to cancel future renewals; access continues through the paid period. Support, membership management and account deletion remain available after your trial ends. Live subscriptions are not yet enabled. Contact Support for billing questions or refund requests.

Beta limitations

Features and availability may change while testing. Keep your own copies of important content. You can edit or delete posts, delete individual stories, block accounts and delete your account from settings. The beta should not be used to store sensitive or essential files.

Contact

Use Support to contact @dgueye1 or email doudousygueye1997@gmail.com. The operator is @dgueye1 in Georgia, United States. Public-launch policy review remains pending.
