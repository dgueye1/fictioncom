FictionCom terms and community rules

Updated October 2, 2026.

Joining FictionCom

FictionCom is an anime and manga community operated by @dgueye1 in Georgia, United States. You must be at least 14 to create an account. Keep your login private and use accurate account information.

Membership and your free trial

You get one seven-day free trial per account. You must add a card through Stripe before the trial starts. At the end of the trial, Stripe automatically charges $5 USD per month, and your membership renews monthly until you cancel.

Cancel before your trial ends to avoid the subscription charge. You can cancel at any time from Profile → Saved & settings → Membership → Cancel subscription or Manage subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; your access continues through the trial or paid period already available to you. Support, membership management and account deletion remain available when your membership expires.

Contact the moderator for billing questions or refund requests. Requests are reviewed individually; this does not limit rights that apply under law. FictionCom does not store your card or bank account numbers.

Posts, stories and chats

Only share content you own or have permission to use. You keep ownership of your content and allow FictionCom to store and display it according to your privacy settings. Do not share harassment, threats, hate, spam, stolen content, or someone else's private information without permission.

Do not upload full nudity, exposed nipples or genitals, explicit sexual activity, or sexual content involving minors or characters depicted as minors. Covered exaggerated anime proportions are allowed. Label spoilers with the series and episode or chapter.

These rules also apply to private chats. You can block users and accept or deny message requests. Contact Support to report an abusive conversation. The moderator can remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules; you can appeal through Support. Uploads publish without automated AI moderation.

Beta availability and help

FictionCom is a beta. Features and availability can change. Keep your own copies of important content and avoid storing sensitive or essential information in the app.

For questions, privacy requests, billing help or appeals, use Support or email doudousygueye1997@gmail.com. You can email even if you cannot sign in. Users under 14 should not register or post.
